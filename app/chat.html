Keyboard

    

      i / a / A / Iinsert before / after / at end / at start
      Escnormal mode; close popup or this help first
      h / l / 0 / $move left / right / line start / line end
      w / bnext word / previous word
      x / X / Ddelete character / previous character / to end
      dw / dd / d$delete word / whole line / to end
      v / Vvisual / visual-line selection
      d / x in visualdelete selection
      y / pyank selection / paste internal clipboard
      uundo
      Tabcomplete commands or transcripts (repeat to cycle); on a bare /news it opens source checkboxes
      Enter in /news pickerload the checked news sources (all are checked by default)
      ↑ / ↓ or k / jmove between composer lines, then previous / next history at the edges
      Enterinsert a newline in the composer (any mode)
      Ctrl/⌘+Entersend question or command (or click Send)
      Ctrl+Ccancel a streaming answer (or copy a selection)
      Ctrl+L / ⌘Kclear the console
      Drag an imageattach a PNG, JPEG, WebP, or GIF to the composer (paste or use the Image button too), then ask a question about it
      Drag textselect output text, then Ctrl+C / ⌘C to copy
      Click a wordopen a streaming /dict popup (drag instead to select); also works in /news, /medium, video summaries, and Project Gutenberg book text
      Click a videoplay it in a centered modal; the right column offers Learn or Chat
      Click a bookopen a /gutenberg result in a two-column reader; the right column answers questions from the book's most relevant paragraphs
      中文 in /newstranslate a news or Medium story title, or the open story, into Simplified Chinese
      ? in codeexplain that line (rust, golang, and php answers)
      − in popupminimize the dictionary popup; click its chip at the right of the status bar to reopen
